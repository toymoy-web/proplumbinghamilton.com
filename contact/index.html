Have this ready when you call

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • What you see or hear, and where in the house it is.
  • Whether you have shut the water off, and where the main shutoff is.
  • The age of the house, and of the water heater if it is involved.
  • Whether you rent or own, and the landlord's contact if you rent.

When to call someone else first

  • You smell gas: leave the house, then call the gas utility's emergency number or 911 from outside.
  • Several neighbors have no water or are backing up: call your water or sewer utility, since the public main may be the problem.
  • Water is near electrical panels or outlets: stay out of the water and call the electric utility or 911 if it is dangerous.
  • Your home needs drying after a flood: use the dedicated line on our water damage restoration page.
