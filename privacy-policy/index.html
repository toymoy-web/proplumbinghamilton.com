Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website or call the phone numbers listed on it, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Call 24/7: (513) 796-9381

Information from phone calls

This site is call-only and has no web forms. When you call a number on this site, your call is handled by a call-routing service that may record your phone number, the time and length of the call, and the page or number you called from. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes, and you may hear a notice to that effect.

Information you give on a call, such as your name, address and a description of the problem, is shared with the independent plumbing or water damage provider who handles your request, so they can contact you and do the work.

Information from website visits

Like most websites, this site and its hosting provider may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds Google Maps, and those services may receive your IP address when they load under their own privacy policies.

How information is used

Information is used to connect you with a service provider, respond to your request, maintain and improve the site, and meet legal obligations. It is not sold as a mailing list.

Your choices

You can choose not to call. You can block cookies in your browser settings. To ask about information associated with your phone number, email contact@proplumbinghamilton.com.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
