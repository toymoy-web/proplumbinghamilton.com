Winter: frozen lines to barns, garages and well houses

Ross Township is mostly rural, with larger lots, outbuildings and long water lines. Those long runs to barns, detached garages and well houses are the first to freeze in a hard cold snap, often in places a homeowner rarely checks.

A frozen pipe in an outbuilding can split and run for days before anyone notices. Our frozen pipe repair page covers thawing and repair. For any line that freezes every winter, rerouting or burying it deeper is usually cheaper than repeated repairs.

  • Drain and shut off lines to outbuildings that are not heated.
  • Insulate or heat-tape any exposed pipe in a well house.
  • Disconnect hoses from outdoor spigots in fall.
  • Know where the shutoff is for every outbuilding line.

Frozen or burst line in Ross Township? Before we arrive

Close the right valve

On a well system, close the valve after the pressure tank. For a split line to a barn or garage, close that branch shutoff if you have one.

Turn off the well pump if water is pouring

Switching off the pump breaker stops it from refilling a broken line and burning out while running dry.

Clear the area and stay warm

Move feed, tools and stored items out of the water. Never thaw a frozen line with a torch in a barn or well house.

Spring and summer: well water and hard water

Homes on private wells draw untreated water from the aquifer. Unlike Hamilton's city water, it is not softened, so scale builds quickly in water heaters and fixtures. Iron is also common in well water, leaving orange staining.

A water softener, often paired with iron filtration, is one of the most common installs in Ross Township. Private wells are not tested by any utility, so a periodic water test is the owner's job.

Well systems: what we handle and what we do not

A well system has more parts than a city connection. There is the pump in the well, a pressure tank, a pressure switch, and often a softener or iron filter. Most everyday problems live in the house end of that chain. A pressure tank that has lost its air charge makes the pump cycle on and off rapidly, and a failing pressure switch can leave the house with no water at all.

We handle the household side: pressure tanks, switches, treatment equipment, water heaters and all the piping in the house. Pulling a pump out of the well or drilling a new well is specialized work, and Ohio's private water system rules in Ohio Administrative Code Chapter 3701-28 require a registered private water systems contractor for it. We will tell you plainly when a call needs one.

Year-round: drains and the move from septic to sewer

Butler County extended sanitary sewers into the downtown Ross service area to replace septic systems, a project reviewed by the EPA in 2014, and the county's Wade Mill Water Reclamation facility serves the area. Homes that switched from septic to sewer sometimes still have old drain lines from the septic era, which can sag, crack or collect roots.

Backups in these homes usually start with drain cleaning and a camera look to see whether the old line should be repaired. Any replacement building sewer is installed and inspected under the Ohio Plumbing Code, which is based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC).
