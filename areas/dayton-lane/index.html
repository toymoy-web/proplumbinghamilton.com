Where Dayton Lane is

The city's street map places Dayton Street, Campbell Avenue, Heaton Street, High Street, Buckeye Street and North 5th through 11th Streets inside Dayton Lane, all with ZIP 45011. It borders German Village and downtown to the west, the North End to the north, and the East End and Jefferson to the south.

Campbell Avenue Park, a one-acre city park at 700 Campbell Avenue, sits in the middle of the neighborhood. Campbell Avenue is named for Lewis D. Campbell, a congressman from Hamilton.

Homes in Dayton Lane

The city describes Dayton Lane as home to the residences of Hamilton's industrial elite from the late 1800s through the early 1900s. Travel Butler County counts more than 200 structures in the historic district, which is listed on the National Register as the Dayton-Campbell Historic District.

City data shows about 68 percent of homes here are rented. Many calls come from landlords managing large older houses as well as from owners restoring them.

Plumbing help Dayton Lane homeowners call about

Large houses mean long pipe runs, several bathrooms and plumbing that has been changed by many owners over the years. These pages cover the work that comes up most.

  • Leak detection to find a hidden leak before opening plaster walls or ceilings.
  • Repiping when an owner decides to replace supply lines throughout the house.
  • Sewer line repair after a camera inspection of the lateral.
  • Water heater replacement sized for a house with several bathrooms.

Planning repairs in a large older house

In a big house, a leak can travel a long way before it shows. Finding the source with acoustic or thermal tools first means one small, planned opening instead of several exploratory ones.

Before larger work, ask for a plan that names which walls, ceilings or floors will be opened and how they will be closed. Replacement piping has to meet the 2024 Ohio Plumbing Code, built on the 2021 IPC, even in a house that predates any code. Work that changes the outside of a building in the historic district is worth checking with the city's planning department first.

City rules that apply in Dayton Lane

Dayton Lane homes are on City of Hamilton water and Hamilton's own gas utility. Hamilton code section 931.031 makes the owner responsible for the sewer lateral, and plumbing permits come from the city's building department.
