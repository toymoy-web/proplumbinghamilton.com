Where Highland Park is

Highland Park sits on Hamilton's west side, between Rossville and Taft Place/Governor's Hill. The city's street map places Park Avenue, Cereal Avenue, Haldimand Avenue, North Dick Avenue, Lawn Avenue, Highland Avenue, Gray Avenue and Emerson Avenue inside it, and most addresses use ZIP 45013.

It also borders Prospect Hill, Armondale and Washington. Virginia Avenue Park, a 3.5-acre city park at Virginia and Gray Avenues, is inside the neighborhood.

Homes in Highland Park

The city says Highland Park was quickly settled by influential business and civic leaders after 1919, with homes designed by local architects Frederick Mueller and George Barkman. The neighborhood combines the Lawn View, Lawn Park and Highland Park subdivisions.

City data shows about 81 percent of homes are owner-occupied, the highest share of the five neighborhoods on our site. Owners who plan to stay often ask about upgrades as well as repairs.

Plumbing help Highland Park homeowners call about

Long-term owners tend to plan work ahead rather than wait for a failure. These pages cover the projects and repairs that come up most.

  • Water heater replacement, including tank versus tankless.
  • Repiping when an owner decides to replace the supply lines.
  • Faucet and sink repair for leaks and fixture upgrades.
  • Shower and tub work during a bathroom remodel.
  • Leak detection before opening a finished wall or ceiling.

Upgrading plumbing without losing what makes the house

When a house has original trim, built-ins or tile you want to keep, plan the route for new pipe before work begins. Ask where the access openings will be and whether they can go inside closets, a basement ceiling or the back of a cabinet.

A written scope that lists each opening and how it will be patched is the easiest way to compare quotes. It also avoids surprises when the work starts. New supply and drain lines go in to the 2024 Ohio Plumbing Code, which uses the 2021 IPC as its base.

City rules that apply in Highland Park

Like the rest of the west side, Highland Park is served by City of Hamilton water and gas. Permits for a water heater swap or a repipe go through the city's building department, and section 931.031 of the city code leaves the sewer lateral with the owner.

Neighbors in Prospect Hill and Rossville work under the same rules.
