Where Rossville is

Rossville is the first neighborhood west of the river from downtown. The city's street map places South B Street, Ross Avenue, Franklin Street, Park Avenue, Main Street and New London Road inside the neighborhood, and almost every address uses ZIP 45013.

It borders Prospect Hill, Highland Park, Armondale and New London. Veterans Park, a 14-acre city park at 20 New London Road, is one of the parks on the city's map for the neighborhood.

A town before it was part of Hamilton

According to the city, Rossville was a separate town from 1804 until it was annexed into Hamilton in 1854. It was named for Senator James Ross, and Ross Avenue was planned as its main street. Several streets were later renamed, such as Morris Street to Main Street and Boudinot Street to Park Avenue.

The Rossville Historic District is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Travel Butler County notes that 19th century Rossville was known for its inns, agricultural businesses, breweries and industry.

Plumbing help for Rossville homes, rentals and shops

City data shows about 63 percent of Rossville homes are rented, so many calls come from landlords and tenants rather than owner-occupants. Main Street businesses call about restrooms, kitchen drains and backflow testing.

  • Commercial plumbing for Main Street restaurants, salons and offices.
  • Drain cleaning for kitchen, laundry and main line clogs.
  • Toilet repair for running, leaking or clogged toilets in homes and apartments.
  • Backflow testing when a business receives its annual test notice.
  • Water heater repair when a unit stops heating or starts leaking.

Plumbing work in a historic district

Most plumbing repairs happen inside the walls and do not change a building's outside appearance. Work that does, such as a new vent through the roof or a new exterior line, is worth checking with the city's planning department before it starts.

In older buildings, ask for the repair plan to say where walls or floors will be opened and how they will be patched. That keeps plaster, trim and original floors in mind before the work begins.

City rules that apply in Rossville

Rossville homes and businesses are on City of Hamilton water and Hamilton's own gas utility. Under Hamilton code section 931.031, the property owner is responsible for the sewer lateral, which for a rental means the landlord.

Plumbing permits come from Hamilton's building department. Next door, Highland Park and Prospect Hill follow the same rules.
