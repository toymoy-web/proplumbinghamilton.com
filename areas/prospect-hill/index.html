Where Prospect Hill is

Prospect Hill is on Hamilton's west side, north of Rossville. The city's street map places North B, C, D, E and F Streets, Webster Avenue, Elvin Avenue, Rhea Avenue, Cereal Avenue, Cleveland Avenue and part of Eaton Avenue inside the neighborhood, and most addresses use ZIP 45013.

It borders Rossville, Highland Park, the North End, Washington and Taft Place/Governor's Hill. Its parks include the 65-acre Combs Park at 1000 North B Street and the 21-acre Jim Grimm Park on Cleveland Avenue, which has a splash ground.

How Prospect Hill was built

According to the city, Peter Thomson bought the former Thomas Rhea farm and filed a plat naming the subdivision Prospect Hill, and he also built the nearby Grandview subdivision. His family later donated the land that is now Twin Run Golf Course.

The city describes the neighborhood as tree-lined and notes its craftsman homes. City data shows about 54 percent of homes are owner-occupied.

Plumbing help Prospect Hill homeowners call about

Prospect Hill has a mix of long-time owners, young families and rentals near the hospital. These pages cover the most common requests.

  • Water heater repair for no hot water, a leaking tank or a pilot that will not stay lit.
  • Drain cleaning for slow tubs, kitchen sinks and main line backups.
  • Frozen pipe repair for lines on outside walls during a cold snap.
  • Toilet repair for running or leaking toilets.

Before you call: three quick checks

A few minutes of checking makes the call shorter and the visit more accurate.

  • Find the main shutoff where the water line enters the basement and make sure it turns.
  • Note which fixtures are affected. One slow drain points to that fixture, while several slow drains point to the main line.
  • Read the water heater's label for its age and size before you call about hot water.

City rules that apply in Prospect Hill

Water at a Prospect Hill tap comes from the city's own wellfields, and gas comes through Hamilton's municipal utility. The lateral from the house to the street main is the owner's to maintain under city code 931.031, and the Hamilton building department issues plumbing permits.

Next door, Highland Park and Rossville share the same west side ZIP and the same rules.
