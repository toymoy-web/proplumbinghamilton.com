Gas Line Installation & Repair in Hamilton, OH

Gas line installation in Hamilton, OH runs new natural gas piping to appliances such as a range, dryer, water heater, fireplace or generator. It also covers repairs to piping that is leaking or damaged. The line starts at the meter and ends at the appliance shutoff valve.

If you smell gas right now, leave the house first and call from outside. For planned gas work, call (513) 796-9381.

Call about a gas line: (513) 796-9381

Smell gas? Do this

  • Leave the house and keep others out.
  • Do not flip switches, use phones inside or light anything.
  • From outside, call the gas utility's emergency number or 911.
  • Do not go back in until the utility says it is safe.

Hamilton's gas comes from its own utility

The City of Hamilton has run its own natural gas utility since 1890. It serves roughly 23,000 customers through about 275 miles of pipeline, and it is the largest municipally owned gas utility in Ohio. The utility's ownership ends at the meter, which it also owns. The piping after the meter belongs to the property owner.

Gas work inside the house must be done under a permit. Hamilton requires a Contractor Registration for gas work, and only the Master Registration holder or their authorized representative can apply for the permit. Gas piping is pressure-tested and inspected before new appliances are connected.

Adding appliances increases total demand, so the whole system is checked, not just the new branch. That is especially true for a tankless water heater.

Signs of a gas leak

  • A rotten egg or sulfur smell near an appliance or the meter.
  • A hissing sound near gas piping.
  • Dead or discolored vegetation in a line along the yard.
  • Bubbles in standing water outside, near a buried gas line.
  • Carbon monoxide alarm sounding, which can signal a venting problem.

Any gas odor gets handled as an emergency, no exceptions. Leave first, call from outside, and let the gas utility or fire department make the house safe before any repair is scheduled.

Materials and why they matter

Gas lines are run in black steel pipe or corrugated stainless steel tubing, known as CSST. CSST is flexible and quicker to install, but manufacturers require it to be bonded to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of damage from lightning. Older installations without that bonding are worth checking.

Flexible appliance connectors also have a service life. An old uncoated brass connector behind a range or dryer should be replaced during any appliance swap. If the job is part of a water heater change, our water heater replacement page covers the rest of that work.

Gas line work in Hamilton and nearby

We install and repair gas piping in ZIP codes 45011, 45013 and 45015. Homes outside the city's gas territory, including parts of Ross Township, may use a different gas supplier or propane, and the same permit and test rules apply.

What gas line work costs depends on

Length and demand drive most of the price. These factors matter most.

  • Distance from the meter or nearest adequate line.
  • Pipe size needed for the total appliance load.
  • Material: black steel or CSST.
  • Access through finished ceilings or walls.
  • Permit, pressure test and inspection.

New gas lines are not covered. Damage from a sudden gas-related event may be, but the piping repair itself usually is not.
