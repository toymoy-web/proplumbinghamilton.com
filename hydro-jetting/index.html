Hydro Jetting in Hamilton, OH

Hydro jetting in Hamilton, OH is a way of cleaning drain and sewer lines with a high-pressure water hose and a spinning nozzle that scours the whole pipe wall. Where a cable punches a hole through a clog, jetting removes the grease, scale and fine roots coating the inside of the pipe.

It is the right tool when a line keeps clogging after it has been cabled. Call (513) 796-9381 to find out if your line is a good candidate.

Jetting is worth a look when

  • The same drain has been cabled more than once this year.
  • A kitchen line is lined with hardened grease.
  • A camera shows fine roots at several clay joints.
  • A cable got stuck partway down the line.

When cabling stops working

Cabling is fast and inexpensive, and for most first-time clogs it is the right call. The trouble comes when the clog returns within weeks or months. That usually means the cable opened a narrow path through buildup that is still stuck to the pipe wall.

Hydro jetting pushes water at several thousand pounds per square inch through a nozzle with rear-facing jets. The jets propel the hose down the line while cutting grease and root hairs off the walls and flushing the debris toward the city main.

For many owners who start with drain cleaning, jetting is the second step when the problem keeps coming back. Jetting runs from a cleanout, and the Ohio Plumbing Code, based on the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), requires accessible cleanouts on the building drain for exactly this kind of maintenance.

The camera comes first

Jetting is powerful, and that power has limits. On a cracked clay section, a separated joint or a fiber pipe that has softened, high pressure can make the damage worse. That is why the line is inspected with a camera before jetting and again after.

If the camera shows a broken pipe, jetting is not the fix. The honest answer is a repair, covered on our sewer line repair page. If the pipe is sound but coated, jetting is often the most cost-effective long-term cleaning.

In older Hamilton neighborhoods, clay laterals often have roots at many joints. Jetting clears them far more completely than a cable, but roots grow back, so it is usually paired with a plan: yearly maintenance or a liner.

Kitchen lines and small commercial kitchens

Grease is the most common reason for jetting in homes. It cools on the pipe wall and builds up in layers, especially on long, flat kitchen runs through a basement. Jetting clears it back to bare pipe.

Restaurants and food businesses deal with the same problem at a larger scale and are also required to maintain grease interceptors. Our commercial plumbing page covers that work.

What hydro jetting costs depends on

Jetting uses specialized equipment, so it costs more than cabling. These factors set the final price.

  • Line length and diameter from the cleanout to the main.
  • Access. A usable cleanout makes the job much faster.
  • Buildup severity. Heavy grease or dense roots take more passes.
  • Camera inspection before and after, priced as its own step.
  • Timing. Scheduled jetting costs less than an emergency visit.

Line cleaning is maintenance and is not covered by insurance. Keep the camera footage, as it helps if you ever need to prove the condition of the line.
